AI Business Agent

Intelligent decision-support SaaS that fuses retrieval-augmented generation with Monte Carlo simulation, giving operations teams a conversational control tower for risk and planning.

Impact

35k+ Stakeholder questions resolved with grounded, explainable answers.
120+ Monte Carlo risk simulations generated weekly with narrative-ready summaries.
92% Sustained accuracy with a 2.7 s median latency across workloads.

Solution Highlights

  • Hybrid RAG pipeline combining pgVector + OpenSearch to ingest PDFs, CSVs, and imagery while retaining lineage.
  • Agentic orchestration around Ollama 3B enforcing deterministic guardrails, safety filters, and audit logs.
  • Monte Carlo engine (10k+ iterations) surfacing scenario KPIs, sensitivity analysis, and alert thresholds.
  • Grafana dashboards stitched with OpenTelemetry traces to monitor latency, hallucination rate, and usage trends.

Tech Stack

Next.js 14 · FastAPI · PostgreSQL + pgVector · OpenSearch · MinIO · Redis · Ollama 3B · Docker Compose · Grafana
